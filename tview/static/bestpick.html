How the pick is selected

The Best Pick is selected from today's scan as the highest-ranked pullback setup that passes all watchlist checks:

  • ✓ Trend OK — weekly & monthly above the 200 SMA, or 5+/6 SMAs bullish (healthy uptrend)
  • ✓ At 100 — daily price within ±3% of the 100 SMA (pulled back to support)
  • ✓ Was high — was ≥5% above the daily 100 SMA within ~4 weeks (genuine dip, not stuck)

Ranking considers the number of bullish moving averages, how close price is to the 100 SMA, and pattern confirmations (hammer candles, pullback streaks, volume behavior, double bottoms).

Entry is the current close price. Stop loss sits just below the recent swing low (or 2×ATR when appropriate). Target is set at 1.2× the risk distance above entry.

Position sizing assumes you risk 1% of account equity to the stop level — a common rule of thumb for retail traders. Adjust to match your own risk rules.

If no stock currently qualifies (e.g. market extended, no clean pullbacks today), the page will say so clearly.
