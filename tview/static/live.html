What is the Live Bounce Tracker?

The Live Bounce Tracker monitors stocks with healthy uptrends (Trend OK) and shows how close they are to bouncing on their daily 200 SMA using real-time 5-minute price data.

What does "Trend OK" mean?

A stock qualifies as Trend OK when:

  • ✓ Weekly & monthly are above the 200 SMA, OR
  • ✓ 5 or more of the 6 SMAs are bullish (daily/weekly/monthly × 100/200 SMA)

How is distance calculated?

Live Price is the latest close from 5-minute intraday bars. Daily 200 SMA is computed from daily bars (not intraday). Distance shows how far the live price sits above (+) or below (−) the daily 200 SMA as a percentage.

Stocks are sorted by absolute distance — closest first. A stock trading at or just below the 200 SMA (e.g. −0.5%) is a potential bounce candidate.

Approach direction

  • ↓ Falling — price is dropping toward the 200 SMA (recent 5-min bars are lower)
  • ↑ Rising — price is bouncing away from the 200 SMA (recent 5-min bars are higher)
  • → Neutral — sideways or not enough bars to judge

Data freshness

Intraday bars are delayed by Yahoo Finance (typically 15 minutes for free data). The Latest Bar column shows the timestamp of the most recent 5-minute bar received. Outside US market hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET), the page will show the last bar from the previous trading session.

Auto-refresh

The page refreshes every 2 minutes while you're viewing it. Since Render's free plan sleeps after inactivity, this tracker only updates while the page is open.
